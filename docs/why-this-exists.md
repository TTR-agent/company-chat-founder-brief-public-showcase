# Why This Exists

This public showcase demonstrates an operating layer for teams where important work is scattered across chat and the founder has to infer what is happening.

The system turns team-chat updates into a founder-ready readout:

- what each person appears to be working on
- where the company has commercial movement
- where ownership or next steps are unclear
- what the founder should ask in meetings
- what cannot be inferred because visibility is too low

It was designed for a company context where communication was not strong enough for the founder to quickly understand execution, blockers, and meeting priorities. The system does not replace management judgment. It gives the founder a clearer operating read before conversations happen.

## What It Improves

- Reduces hidden work and vague status updates.
- Makes commercial impact visible.
- Turns scattered chat into a short management brief.
- Gives the founder meeting-ready questions.
- Keeps low-evidence situations honest instead of inventing progress.
- Preserves a consistent founder voice so meeting prep feels natural.

## What It Does Not Do

- It does not read private Slack data in this public version.
- It does not include real employee names, messages, channels, or API tokens.
- It does not score people as employees.
- It does not automate HR decisions.
- It does not post to Slack or send messages.
